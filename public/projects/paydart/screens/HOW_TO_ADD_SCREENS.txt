PAYDART MOBILE UI SCREENSHOTS

Export the application screens as portrait PNG or WebP files. A 9:19.5 aspect ratio works best.

Suggested filenames:
- login.webp
- student-overview.webp
- upcoming-fees.webp
- additional-fees.webp
- payment-history.webp
- digital-receipt.webp

After adding the files here, open src/data/projects.ts and set the matching PayDart image value.

Example:
image: "/projects/paydart/screens/login.webp"
